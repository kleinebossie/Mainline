## 1. Ethical Growth Principles

Mainline grows through radical transparency, word-of-mouth recommendations, and scientific credibility.

### Core Growth Tenets

- **No Dark Patterns**: Never use streak shaming, artificial scarcity, manipulative notifications, or deceptive discounts.
- **No Extractive Advertising**: The application contains zero advertisements and sells no user data.
- **Free Core Training**: All training science, assessment tools, and adaptation algorithms remain free for all users forever.
- **Developer Patronage**: Monetization uses optional subscription patronage to fund server compute, APIs, and direct development.
- **Radical Honesty as Differentiation**: Clearly explain that no chess training method has proven causal rating gains. Trust is the primary moat.

## 2. Beta Cohort Management

Beta invitations scale in controlled cohorts to protect operational stability and support responsiveness.

### Cohort Progression

1. **Pilot Cohort (Stage A)**:
   - Target: 5 to 10 active chess players across different rating bands (<1200, 1200-1600, 1600+).
   - Goal: Validate onboarding clarity, platform account connection, and bug reporting channels.
2. **Research Cohort (Stage B)**:
   - Target: 30 to 50 active players with dedicated OTB and online training routines.
   - Goal: Collect longitudinal adherence data and observe natural training patterns under informed consent.
3. **Public Beta Cohort (Stage C)**:
   - Target: Open community adoption through chess forums, blogs, and word-of-mouth.
   - Goal: Test infrastructure scalability, automated error bounds, and patronage subscription adoption.

## 3. Sustainable Adherence and Engagement

Mainline approaches user retention through Self-Determination Theory rather than behavioral addiction loops.

### Retention Framework

- **Autonomy**: Users retain full control over training constraints, weekly focus choices, and session durations.
- **Competence**: Progress dashboards highlight controllable effort, mistake reductions, and tactical accuracy rather than volatile rating fluctuations.
- **Light-Touch Feedback**: Solicit user feedback once per week or contextually after repeatedly difficult drills. Never interrupt active training flows with intrusive surveys.
- **Cognitive Firewall**: Protect users from rating obsession by centering daily process metrics instead of rating changes.
